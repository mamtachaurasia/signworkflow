## 💡 Content Creation Strategy by Keyword Type

### Volume Keywords (High Search Volume)
**Strategy:** Create comprehensive guides that target multiple related keywords
**Example:** "The Complete Guide to E-Signatures for [Industry]"
- URL: `/solutions/by-industry/[industry]` (Already targeting this!)
- Content: Covers all main questions and pain points
- SEO Weight: Can rank for 50+ related keywords

### Long-tail Keywords (Lower Volume, Higher Intent)
**Strategy:** Create specific blog posts targeting specific pain points
**Example:** "How to Reduce [Industry] Contract Approval Time from 30 to 5 Days"
- URL: `/blog/[industry]-contract-approval-acceleration`
- Content: Specific use case with actionable steps
- Conversion Potential: Much higher conversion rate

### Question Keywords
**Strategy:** Create FAQ sections and how-to content
**Example:** "What is the best e-signature for [Industry]?"
- URL: `/solutions/by-industry/[industry]#faq` (with schema markup)
- Content: Direct answer with comparison
- Feature: Likely to appear in position zero (featured snippet)
