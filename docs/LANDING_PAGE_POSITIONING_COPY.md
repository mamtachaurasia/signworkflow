## Comparison Messaging

Suggested positioning language:

- simpler than enterprise CLM
- more controlled than lightweight e-sign tools
- built for growing businesses, not only enterprise procurement cycles

Avoid over-claiming language like:

- best in the world
- replaces every contract platform
- enterprise-grade everything

Better alternatives:

- designed for growing teams
- practical for compliance-aware workflows
- built for shared business operations
